Facts: LLMs generate one token at a time; for each position the model outputs a probability for every possible next
token and the sampler picks one (temperature / top-p), appends it and repeats. Sampling is why the same prompt can give
different answers. Trained on a very large text corpus. (Simplified: words stand in for tokens.)

ChatGPT has no idea what it's about to say. Not even the next word.
Every time it answers you, it plays one tiny game, over and over.
It reads everything so far, and asks one question. What word probably comes next?
Say you typed, the cat sat on the... Mat gets a big slice of the wheel. Sofa gets a smaller one. Moon gets a tiny sliver.
Then it spins. Picks one word. Sticks it on the end. And spins again. For every single word.
You see, it's like a gambler who's watched a billion games. He can't see the future, but he's scary good at betting on it.
That's why the same question can get a different answer. The wheel just landed somewhere else.
So next time AI sounds super sure of itself, remember. It's just on a hot streak.
